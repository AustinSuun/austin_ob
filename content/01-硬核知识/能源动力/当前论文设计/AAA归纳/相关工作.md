# 2. Related Work

### 2.1 3D Object Detection on Point Clouds

3D object detection is a fundamental task in autonomous perception. Existing methodologies are largely defined by their data representation strategies. Voxel-based methods [?,?] discretize point clouds into regular 3D grids or pillars to leverage efficient Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs). While computationally efficient, they inevitably suffer from information loss due to quantization artifacts. Point-based methods [?,?], exemplified by PointNet++ [?], directly process raw points to preserve fine-grained geometric details. However, they incur high computational overhead from neighbor searching and grouping operations, limiting their scalability to large-scale scenes.

Recently, Transformer-based architectures [?,?] have achieved state-of-the-art performance by modeling long-range dependencies via self-attention mechanisms. Despite their success, the quadratic complexity $O(N^2)$ of attention maps poses a significant bottleneck for processing dense LiDAR scenes containing hundreds of thousands of points. This trade-off between global modeling capability and computational efficiency motivates the exploration of State Space Models (SSMs) in the 3D domain.

### 2.2 State Space Models and Serialization

Originating from control theory, Structured State Space Models (SSMs) [?,?] map a 1D sequence $x(t) \to y(t)$ through a latent state $h(t)$. The recently introduced Mamba [?] architecture incorporates a selective scanning mechanism, achieving input-dependent dynamics with linear complexity $O(N)$.

Adaptation from 2D to 3D. Initial adaptations of Mamba for vision, such as Vim [?] and VMamba [?], focused on 2D images. Since images possess a dense, regular grid structure, these methods employ 2D-specific strategies—such as raster scanning or concentric cross-scans—to flatten pixels. Following this trajectory, pioneering 3D works like PointMamba [?] and PCM [?] extend these paradigms to 3D space, predominantly utilizing Space-Filling Curves (SFCs) like Hilbert curves [?] or Z-order curves [?] to organize points based on spatial coordinates.

Limitations of Static Geometric Ordering. While SFCs work well for dense 2D grids, directly applying them to 3D point clouds introduces structural issues due to sparsity and irregularity.

- **Sparsity Mismatch:** Unlike dense images, 3D space is highly sparse. A fixed geometric curve must traverse vast empty regions to connect distant valid points, introducing noise and interrupting feature continuity.
    
- Semantic Blindness: Geometric orderings operate independently of semantic content. For example, a Z-order curve might alternate between a "car roof" and the "ground" due to vertical adjacency, breaking the semantic coherence required for effective feature learning.
    
    Proposed: Salience-driven Dynamic Serialization. To address these limitations, we propose Gravity Streamline Mapping (GSM). Unlike rigid geometric paths, GSM is content-aware. It interprets feature magnitude as a proxy for semantic importance (salience), generating a traversal path aligned with feature density gradients. This ensures that the sequence clusters semantically related points (e.g., traversing an object foreground) before processing background context, maximizing the information density of the input sequence.
    

### 2.3 Structure Completion and Densification

LiDAR point clouds inherently suffer from distance-dependent sparsity and are limited to object surfaces. Previous studies [?] have observed that this "surface-only" characteristic creates a spatial distribution gap between the captured points and the object's actual volumetric center.

Incomplete Geometric Representation. Since LiDAR rays reflect off the exterior, the internal volume of objects remains hollow. This lack of internal structure restricts the backbone's ability to extract holistic features, as the model must infer the object's full shape solely from its boundary shell. This issue is particularly pronounced for sparse or occluded objects, where surface cues are fragmentary.

Internal Structure Recovery. While some works employ generative completion networks [?,?] for reconstruction, they are often computationally heavy and separate from the detection pipeline. In contrast, our Ray-Gravity Guided Expansion (RGGE) module integrates densification directly into the backbone. Motivated by the need to recover volumetric context, RGGE extends surface points inward along the LiDAR ray direction under local geometric constraints. This process effectively fills internal voids, mitigating the spatial bias of surface sampling and providing a more complete geometric representation for downstream feature learning.

### 中文对照参考

2.1 3D 点云目标检测

3D 目标检测是自动驾驶感知的基本任务。现有的方法主要由其数据表示策略定义。基于体素的方法 [?,?] 将点云离散化为规则的 3D 网格或柱体，以利用高效的卷积神经网络（CNN）。虽然计算效率高，但它们不可避免地因量化伪影而遭受信息丢失。基于点的方法 [?,?]，以 PointNet++ [?] 为代表，直接处理原始点以保留细粒度的几何细节。然而，它们因邻域搜索和分组操作而产生高昂的计算开销，限制了其在大规模场景中的扩展性。

最近，基于 Transformer 的架构 [?,?] 通过自注意力机制对长程依赖进行建模，取得了最先进的性能。尽管取得了成功，但注意力图的 $O(N^2)$ 二次复杂度构成了处理包含数十万个点的密集 LiDAR 场景的重大瓶颈。这种在全局建模能力和计算效率之间的权衡，促使了在 3D 领域对状态空间模型（SSMs）的探索。

2.2 状态空间模型与序列化

源于控制理论的结构化状态空间模型（SSMs）[?,?] 通过潜在状态 $h(t)$ 映射 1D 序列 $x(t) \to y(t)$。最近推出的 Mamba [?] 架构引入了选择性扫描机制，以线性复杂度 $O(N)$ 实现了输入依赖的动态特性。

从 2D 到 3D 的适应。 Mamba 在视觉领域的初步应用，如 Vim [?] 和 VMamba [?]，主要集中在 2D 图像上。由于图像具有稠密、规则的网格结构，这些方法采用了 2D 特定的策略——如光栅扫描或同心交叉扫描——来展平像素。沿着这一轨迹，先驱性的 3D 工作如 PointMamba [?] 和 PCM [?] 将这些范式扩展到了 3D 空间，主要使用空间填充曲线（SFCs）如 希尔伯特曲线 [?] 或 Z 序曲线 [?]，根据空间坐标来组织点。

静态几何排序的局限性。 虽然 SFCs 对稠密 2D 网格有效，但直接将其应用于 3D 点云会因 稀疏性 和 不规则性 而引入结构性问题。

- **稀疏性不匹配：** 与稠密图像不同，3D 空间高度稀疏。固定的几何曲线必须穿越巨大的空白区域来连接远处的有效点，从而引入噪声并中断特征连续性。
    
- 语义盲目性： 几何排序独立于语义内容运行。例如，Z 序曲线可能会仅仅因为垂直相邻就在“车顶”和“地面”之间交替扫描，破坏了有效特征学习所需的语义连贯性。
    
    提出的方法：显著性驱动的动态序列化。 为了解决这些局限性，我们提出了 重力流线映射 (GSM)。与僵硬的几何路径不同，GSM 是内容感知的。它将特征幅度解释为语义重要性（显著性）的代理，生成一条与 特征密度梯度 对齐的遍历路径。这确保了序列在处理背景上下文之前聚类语义相关的点（例如，遍历物体前景），从而最大化输入序列的信息密度。
    

2.3 结构补全与稠密化

LiDAR 点云本质上受到距离相关稀疏性的影响，且仅限于物体表面。先前的研究 [?] 观察到，这种 “仅表面（Surface-only）” 的特性在采集到的点与物体实际的体积中心之间产生了空间分布差距。

不完整的几何表示。 由于 LiDAR 射线从外部反射，物体的内部体积保持为空洞。这种内部结构的缺失限制了骨干网络提取整体特征的能力，因为模型必须仅凭其边界外壳来推断物体的完整形状。这一问题在稀疏或被遮挡的物体中尤为明显，表面线索本身就是残缺的。

内部结构恢复。 虽然一些工作采用生成式补全网络 [?,?] 进行重建，但它们往往计算量大，且独立于检测流程之外。相比之下，我们的 射线重力引导的拓展 (RGGE) 模块将稠密化直接集成到骨干网络中。受恢复体积上下文需求的启发，RGGE 在 局部几何约束 下沿激光雷达射线方向向内延伸表面点。这一过程有效地 填充了内部空洞，缓解了表面采样的空间偏差，并为下游的特征学习提供了更完整的几何表示。
